# Solvency II reconciliation and actuarial analysis of change — acceptance specification

**Revision 3 · 27 November 2026 · Scope change instructed; numerical/platform decisions pending.** A Head of Actuarial Function must be able to review the files in Microsoft 365 Excel, reproduce the reported reconciliation and assess the insurer’s actuarial analysis of change (AoC). Reconciliation alone does not establish actuarial causes. This revision preserves R-001–R-037, adds R-038–R-055 and records the owner’s new instruction as D10. D11–D22 require owner answers before dependent algorithms or release claims are accepted. Revision 2 remains in Git history; the original user story is unchanged.

| **D5 · Solvency ratio** (R-011, R-021, R-032) | The owner confirmed that valid filings have positive SCR and selected three separate effects. With opening 0 and closing 1, let ΔE = E₁ − E₀ and Δr = 1/S₁ − 1/S₀. In ratio units, **own funds** = ΔE/S₀, **SCR** = E₀ × Δr, and **interaction** = ΔE × Δr. Their sum equals E₁/S₁ − E₀/S₀. Multiply each effect by 100 for percentage points and round only for display. An input with missing, zero or negative SCR fails validation for the ratio calculation. R-048 applies the same identity to adjacent fully known AoC steps; partial chains never acquire a complete attribution claim. |
| **D6 · Evidence for bridge claims** (R-012–R-019, R-024, R-031) | For reported-data reconciliation, show observed financial comparisons and arithmetic reproducible from cited facts. For linked business, show the reported components and their signed differences. An unexplained difference remains visible. Where the source combines components, show the reported aggregate and mark the unsupported split unavailable. For tier limits, compare available and eligible amounts at both dates: restricted Tier 1 against total Tier 1, and Tier 3 and Tier 2+3 against SCR. The restricted Tier 1 limit is an owner addition to the story. Step 2 confirms from the official text and the S.23.01 instructions how amounts above that limit are reported before any rule relies on that treatment. ### Release scoring protocol (D9)

**Benchmark.** Before running the five cases (six packages, seven scored runs), the owner signs off an independently prepared answer key covering expected bridge lines, signs, stops, unavailable detail and seeded defects. Include at least 20 separately scored movements whose causes cannot be determined from the filings. Count how many receive the required abstention. For example, 19 correct out of 20 meets 95%. A missing response counts as a miss. Check invented numbers and unlabelled causal claims separately; either fails its gate.

**Original triage reviewer trial (R-035; retained).** This protocol measures triage, not AoC utility. D21/R-054 govern any additional AoC trial. Eight practising life actuaries each review two different synthetic cases of matched difficulty, one using the plain Excel diff and the other using the tool. Each case contains seeded defects in both classes. Assign two reviewers to each sequence: A/tool then B/Excel, A/Excel then B/tool, B/tool then A/Excel, and B/Excel then A/tool. This gives each case and method equal representation in each position. Give reviewers the same task and underlying inputs for each case, withhold the answer key and give no feedback between runs.

Start timing when the reviewer opens the case materials and stop when they submit their first written list of issues for follow-up. For each reviewer calculate (Excel time − tool time) / Excel time. The median of the eight reductions must reach **35%**. Separately for each defect class, divide correctly identified seeded defects by opportunities presented under each method. The tool must match or exceed the Excel detection rate in **both** classes. A scorer uses the frozen key on anonymized findings with method labels removed. The owner signs off the times, counts and outcome; an incomplete trial leaves the gate open.

The AoC adds evidence of the insurer's attributed causes to the existing reported-figure reconciliation. Operational risk, LAC-TP, LAC-DT, add-ons and RFF/MAP adjustments retain their separate scope when reconciling basic to total SCR. S.12.01 LoB and guarantee-group detail is supported only at the granularity actually reported or explicitly supplied by the insurer. No risk-margin guarantee split, unreported recoverable split or module-to-LoB allocation is invented.

### Sequential controls, SCR, tiering and ratio

For each measure/segment chain, display these **independent** controls: AoC opening minus reported opening; each stated step start minus the previous supplied step end; AoC closing minus reported closing; and AoC net change minus the existing reported reconciliation change. The signed residual is `reported closing − reported opening − sum(validated signed step impacts)` and remains a C11 line. Show separate continuity breaks even if they cancel in the total residual. Opening/closing differences, unassigned amounts and approximation gaps cannot be folded into experience, economic variance or post-model adjustment. Failed source/continuity checks withhold dependent attribution while keeping unaffected reported reconciliation available. If an expected category has no evidence, display its missing-input status; do not create a zero-impact run. “Not applicable” requires a cited insurer declaration and is distinct from zero and unavailable.

The residual pass/fail applies only to a complete validated chain. If a required step is unavailable, the complete residual is also unavailable with that input named; an optional `reported change minus known steps` comparison may still show the **partial unallocated gap**, explicitly without a complete-chain pass. Missing steps are never treated as zero, even if this partial gap happens to be zero.

R-019 remains a reported accounting reconciliation. Its gross standalone module charges and reported diversification line must be labelled as such. Under the recommended Euler method, provide a separate date allocation of basic SCR, with the reconstruction controls above. Differences between date allocations sum to the recomputed basic-SCR change but remain changes in **allocated** amounts. The actuarial impact of step k is `diversified SCR_after − diversified SCR_before` from its full SCR rerun, including declared operational/LAC/add-on/portfolio adjustments on a compatible basis. A proxy has its own status, method and scope and cannot be promoted to a full rerun or exact diversified attribution; no SCR means `Unavailable: SCR rerun for <step/run>`.

Own-funds eligibility depends on the same-step SCR and all applicable tier restrictions. Recommendation for missing full intermediate SCR: show the chain of **available** own funds by tier and a separate opening/closing reported eligibility reconciliation, with the net tier-eligibility adjustment as a final **reconciling effect**. Do not attribute that final net effect to an earlier actuarial cause. Intermediate eligible tiers, binding/released conclusions and ratio effects then remain unavailable/indeterminate. Where complete reruns and classification evidence do exist, the owner may instead approve stepwise eligibility; no reclassified restricted Tier 1 amount is invented, and D6's unresolved treatment still blocks its attribution.

This alternative uses `change in eligible funds = change in available funds + change in (eligible minus available)`, separately by compatible tier and total. It does not splice an opening available-funds level into a closing eligible-funds chain. The final reconciling effect is a **change in the observed adjustment**, not the entire closing deduction or a proved attribution to a single limit.

For adjacent endpoints k−1 and k with known validated eligible own funds E and positive full SCR S, retain D5 exactly:

- Own funds: `100 × (E_k − E_(k−1)) / S_(k−1)` percentage points.
- SCR: `100 × E_(k−1) × (1/S_k − 1/S_(k−1))` percentage points.
- Interaction: `100 × (E_k − E_(k−1)) × (1/S_k − 1/S_(k−1))` percentage points.

Each step's three effects sum exactly to its ratio change before display rounding; a continuous fully known chain telescopes to the endpoint change. The grouped per-step own-funds/SCR/interaction sums need not equal the three **single endpoint** D5 effects: allocation across a path is order-dependent. Present those as separate schedules with their own definitions. When any intermediate eligible E/full S is absent or proxy-based, mark the affected step terms unavailable and do not claim that the partial terms explain the total. Show the standalone endpoint D5 decomposition if its original checks pass, plus the visible unallocated ratio gap and which boundaries lack evidence. A gap between endpoints may be labelled as a combined observed interval comparison, never split into the missing causes. Missing/nonpositive S is a validation failure, not a division error. Do not add a linear or Shapley alternative.

`Raw_AoC` and `Raw_reference` (or clearly identified equivalent raw tabs) hold original ledger-backed input values, run metadata, curve/matrix parameters and conversion evidence.
