# Verification of Solvency II 2.8.2 source mappings

**Status: preliminary; the Step 2 gate has not passed.** The coordinates in `2.8.2/cells.csv` are source inventory entries, not approval to use every cell in a calculation. Cell-specific signs and many subtotal relationships are still unverified, and some like-for-like equations require further source work. No bridge definitions, fixtures or tool code are supplied. The owner-reviewed [specification, Revision 2](../docs/spec.md), governs where it differs from the user story.

## Sources and version

Every downloaded source is named, dated where known, linked and SHA-256 hashed in [reference/manifest.csv](../reference/manifest.csv). In this document:

- **AT** is `eiopa_sii_2.8.2_annotated.xlsx`, the official annotated templates published 15 October 2024. Its `Table of Contents` identifies the annual solo supervisory entry point **ars** and the annual solo public disclosure entry point **aps**. A cell's exact sheet and Excel location are in `cells.csv`.
- **HF** is the 30 June 2025 optional NACE 2.1 hotfix annotated workbook and associated hotfix release notes/change log. EIOPA describes the hotfix as applicable from annual 2025 for undertakings needing NACE 2.1. The hotfix and base workbooks were compared at the 2,531 mapped physical cells: their blank values and fills match. Across the mapped sheets, the only differences in non-empty cell values are the Article 112 axis annotation at D4 in 15 S.25.01/S.26 sheets, which adds “[root member included]”. The mapping's base `axis_context` must therefore be read with the corresponding hotfix annotation for those sheets. The comparison does not establish that all taxonomy dictionaries or validations are identical.
- **BV** is `eiopa_sii_2.8.2_validations.xlsx`, updated 10 November 2026. Rule identifiers and worksheet row numbers below are from `Business Validation 2.8.2`. `Identical datapoints 2.8.2` is the source of `variant_equivalences.csv`. Record the rule's deactivation status when using it.
- **ITS-R** and **ITS-D** are the original Official Journal PDFs for Implementing Regulations (EU) 2023/894 and 2023/895 respectively. Page references below are **PDF pages**, which for ITS-R also correspond to its OJ L 120 page numbers. The original acts are evidence for the 2.8.2 release; the applicable amendments for each reference date still need a full legal-version check.
- **FR** is the EIOPA XBRL Filing Rules PDF, last updated 3 January 2025; **EX** is the official 2.8.2 instance-examples ZIP. EX files are synthetic examples, not evidence of a real filing. **DPM** is the official 2.8.2 DPM dictionary.
- **L** is the English consolidated text of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/35 effective 14 November 2024, the EIOPA Single Rulebook Article 82 page and the original 2015 OJ PDF. The EUR-Lex version index lists the next consolidation as 30 January 2027. The text was checked as at 25 November 2026; the 31 December 2026 reference date was still in the future.

AT includes 34 relevant sheets with row/column data and one supplementary S.01.02 PEPP sheet with no fixed row-code grid. The inventory contains 2,531 physical cells and 631 EIOPA-identified supervisory/public equivalent pairs. These counts include supplementary rows in those sheets. They do not establish that every required bridge, sign, subtotal, or public disclosure has been verified. The current extraction identifies 38 exact same-column subtotal relationships; the remaining entries have `subtotal_status=unverified`, not “no subtotal”. Most monetary entries still have a general sign rule or an explicitly unverified disclosure sign.

## 3 — Article 82 and restricted Tier 1 (R-017, R-018)

The [full English Article 82 text in the 14 November 2024 consolidation](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/02015R0035-20241114.ENG.xhtml#art_82) is also retained in `reference/` and recorded in the manifest; the original 2015/35 OJ text is on PDF page 59. The EIOPA rulebook says Tier 3 is “less than 15 %”, Tier 2 plus Tier 3 must “not exceed 50 %”, and the listed restricted Tier 1 items are “less than 20 %” of total Tier 1. `2.8.2/legal_limits.csv` records the complete comparisons:

| Article | Comparison | Bound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 82(1)(a) | Eligible Tier 1 ≥ 50% of SCR | Inclusive minimum |
| 82(1)(b) | Eligible Tier 3 < 15% of SCR | Strict maximum |
| 82(1)(c) | Eligible Tier 2 + Tier 3 ≤ 50% of SCR | Inclusive maximum |
| 82(2)(a) | Eligible Tier 1 ≥ 80% of MCR | Inclusive minimum |
| 82(2)(b) | Eligible Tier 2 ≤ 20% of MCR | Inclusive maximum |
| 82(3)(a)–(d) | Relevant restricted Tier 1 items < 20% of total Tier 1, within the limits in 82(1)(a) and 82(2)(a) | Strict maximum |

Paragraph 3 identifies the Article 69(a)(iii), 69(a)(v), 69(b) and Directive 2009/138/EC Article 308b(9) transitional items. **Date status:** the 14 November 2024 English consolidation, Article 82, agrees with these bounds; the EUR-Lex version index lists the next consolidation as 30 January 2027. The 2024 and 2025 annual reference dates fall in that interval. The 2026 year end was still in the future on the research date, so check for any subsequent change before treating it as verified for that date.

ITS-R S.23.01 pp. 817–818 describes R0500/C0030 as available restricted Tier 1, R0500/C0040 as available Tier 2, R0540/C0030 as eligible restricted Tier 1 and R0540/C0040 as eligible Tier 2. The same data points are in the public S.23 table. Those descriptions do **not** separately identify a field for restricted-Tier-1 excess reclassified to Tier 2. The consolidated [Article 73(1)(j)](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/02015R0035-20241114.ENG.xhtml#art_73) describes an alternative Tier 2 feature for the specified Article 69 items exceeding the Article 82(3) limit. EIOPA Guidelines on classification of own funds, Guideline 20 §§1.75(b) and 1.76(b), PDF p. 18, describe excess restricted Tier 1 as “available as Tier 2 basic own funds” for SCR and MCR eligibility calculations. EIOPA Q&A 3519 says it may count as eligible Tier 2 subject to conditions and headroom; its status is **Rejected** because EIOPA considered the issue already addressed by the regulation and Guideline 20. **Unverified:** exactly how that excess is allocated and independently identifiable in the S.23 cells from the S.23 instructions alone. Obtain an explicit EIOPA clarification and validate any inferred attribution before Step 7. An available-versus-eligible difference can also reflect another restriction or rounding.

Guideline 20 §1.76(c) describes Tier 2 eligibility for the MCR as less than 20%, whereas Article 82(2)(b) says it must not exceed 20%. The bounds in `legal_limits.csv` follow the regulation's inclusive wording. The difference should be resolved with authoritative guidance before relying on an equality-boundary conclusion.

## 6 — Reported solvency ratio and precision

The ratio is S.23.01.01.01 **R0620/C0010**, AT A37/D37/I37; the metric is `s2md_met:pi548`, a pure ratio. R0540/C0010 and R0580/C0010 are its reported numerator and denominator. It is common to ars and aps. ITS-R Article 1(b), PDF p. 3, says percentages are expressed “per unit with four decimals”. FR III.9 p. 16 specifies at least four decimals of accuracy for ratio/percentage facts and FR III.8–9 specifies decimal rather than percent notation.

**Owner decision on D3:** for XBRL, use the ratio fact's declared `decimals` precision, rather than the number of lexical digits. For `decimals="4"`, one unit of that precision is 0.0001 in ratio units, or 0.01 percentage point. EX `random/ars_2024-10-15_instance.xbrl` contains `pi548` with `decimals="4"` and more lexical digits, demonstrating why the distinction matters. Preserve the source value and compare with the unrounded ratio as D3 requires.

**Unverified edge case:** the treatment of `decimals="INF"`, absent precision, and a normalized workbook with no stated ratio precision needs an explicit acceptance rule before those cases can produce a D3 pass/fail. Do not infer a finite tolerance from display formatting. Confirm whether INF is admissible for the particular ratio under the applicable filing rules before resolving it.
