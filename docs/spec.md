# Solvency II movement reconciliation — v1 acceptance specification

**Step 1 · Owner review recorded · Revision 2 (25 November 2026).** A Head of Actuarial Function should be able to open the workbook, reproduce a movement from the two submissions, and see which sign-off questions the submissions leave unanswered. The numbered requirements describe observable outcomes. The decision record below explains how the owner’s answers qualify the original story and identifies the work reserved for later steps.

## Scope

| | Boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Users and inputs | A solo Head of Actuarial Function, reporting manager or independent actuarial reviewer supplies two consecutive annual periods for one European life undertaking on the Standard Formula. Inputs are Solvency II XBRL reporting packages or normalized SFCR/QRT workbooks. |
| Template coverage per period | Required: S.02.01 and S.23.01. Expected: S.12.01 and S.25.01. Metadata: S.01.01 and S.01.02 or equivalent XBRL context evidence. Optional enrichment: S.05.01, S.22.01 and S.26.01–S.26.03. Both periods must use EIOPA Solvency II taxonomy 2.8.2. Taxonomy 2.10.0 remains a future design target. |
| Deterministic release (v0.1) | Local Python processing. Outputs are a formula-driven Excel reconciliation workbook and a structured JSON summary of bridges and rule-based flags. The reviewer sees comparability and consistency results before relying on movements. |
| Optional commentary release (v0.2) | An explicit opt-in enables a model provider to receive verified financial figures and draft the Pre-Sign-Off Challenge Pack. The required deliverable is a Word document (.docx) with a one-to-two-page narrative for the actuary, supported by citations and specific review questions. |
| Excluded from v1 | Group consolidation (Methods 1 and 2, including S.32.01), full or partial Internal Model SCR, quarterly filings, PDF SFCR scraping, and analysis of S.29.01–S.29.04. Presence of S.29 is reported. Driver attribution beyond what the reported facts establish belongs to the reviewer. |
| Release evidence | Before a v1.0 tag or public demo, the automated benchmark must pass and the owner must sign off the blinded human trial. The build supplies the materials for that trial. |

## Decisions after owner review

| Decision | Acceptance rule and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| **D1 · Filing scope** (R-001–R-007) | The owner selected **2.8.2** for the first release. Use successive annual reference dates for the same in-scope undertaking and identify the revision supplied for each date. Step 2 records the exact official package and any verified hotfix coverage. Both normalized workbooks and XBRL remain in scope. Mixed-format pairs are unconfirmed and carry no acceptance claim until the input work establishes equivalence. |
| **D2 · Hard stops** (R-003–R-006) | Different entities, currencies or perimeters, out-of-scope filings, and missing required evidence stay blocked. A reviewer may correct metadata with source evidence or explicitly confirm a scale conversion. Retain the original values, factor, resulting units and reason, rerun the gate, and record the outcome in the workbook and JSON. An acknowledgement alone cannot clear a hard stop. Benchmark case 2a scores both the stop and the rerun after a confirmed conversion (R-031). |
| **D3 · Rounding and consistency failures** (R-007–R-012, R-016–R-021) | Compare like-for-like scope and gross/net basis. For money, pass a discrepancy up to and including one declared reporting unit. After a confirmed scale conversion, use one unit of the coarser source precision expressed in the common unit. For example, if one source reports euros and the other thousands of euros, the tolerance on that comparison is €1,000. For the solvency ratio reported in S.23.01, pass a discrepancy up to and including one unit in the last decimal place the filing reports for that ratio, against the ratio recomputed from the reported amounts and not rounded. Show the tolerance and actual signed discrepancy even when it passes. A failed comparison withholds its dependent deltas. Other valid results remain available. |
| **D4 · Review outputs** (R-006, R-009, R-025–R-030) | The owner requires a **Word narrative** in the optional commentary release. Its reader gets cited observations, review questions, labeled hypotheses and concrete follow-up checks. Workbook and JSON remain the default outputs. Editorial defaults are to use the Excel summary as the dashboard, to judge the one-to-two-page narrative in Word, and to include an appendix in the same document only if needed to cover every material item. Markdown is optional. |
| **D5 · Solvency ratio** (R-011, R-021, R-032) | The owner confirmed that valid filings have positive SCR and selected three separate effects. With opening 0 and closing 1, let ΔE = E₁ − E₀ and Δr = 1/S₁ − 1/S₀. In ratio units, **own funds** = ΔE/S₀, **SCR** = E₀ × Δr, and **interaction** = ΔE × Δr. Their sum equals E₁/S₁ − E₀/S₀. Multiply each effect by 100 for percentage points and round only for display. An input with missing, zero or negative SCR fails validation for the ratio calculation. |
| **D6 · Evidence for bridge claims** (R-012–R-019, R-024, R-031) | Show only observed financial comparisons and arithmetic reproducible from their cited facts. For linked business, show the reported components and their signed differences. An unexplained difference remains visible. Where the source combines components, show the reported aggregate and mark the unsupported split unavailable. For tier limits, compare available and eligible amounts at both dates: restricted Tier 1 against total Tier 1, and Tier 3 and Tier 2+3 against SCR. The restricted Tier 1 limit is an owner addition to the story. Step 2 confirms from the official text and the S.23.01 instructions how amounts above that limit are reported before any rule relies on that treatment. If reporting precision or other restrictions prevent a binding-limit conclusion, report the observed values and mark the conclusion indeterminate. |
| **D7 · Evidence for flags** (R-006, R-014–R-015, R-018, R-023–R-024) | Compare reported S.05.01 premiums or claims with the BEL movement and name the accounting measures used. Those comparisons cannot establish actual cash receipts or payments. Keep any possible drivers in the optional narrative as labeled hypotheses with specific checks. Set thresholds in the benchmark step after mapping the available measures. Record units, signs, denominator, equality and zero-base treatment and freeze the settings before scoring. The owner has not supplied numerical cutoffs. |
| **D8 · Traceability** (R-004, R-010, R-022, R-026–R-030, R-033) | Check a reported value against its source fact. Reproduce a derived value from all its cited inputs, with units, signs and precision. Add context, dimensions or workbook locators when the four-part coordinate is ambiguous. Cite header metadata at its actual location. Trace thresholds and legal limits to their rule sources. An unverifiable figure withholds the affected output. |
| **D9 · Release scoring** (R-031–R-036) | The owner delegated the trial design and retains release sign-off. Use the protocol below. The 35% improvement and 95% abstention recall are acceptance targets until measured. |

### Release scoring protocol (D9)

**Benchmark.** Before running the five cases (six packages, seven scored runs), the owner signs off an independently prepared answer key covering expected bridge lines, signs, stops, unavailable detail and seeded defects. Include at least 20 separately scored movements whose causes cannot be determined from the filings. Count how many receive the required abstention. For example, 19 correct out of 20 meets 95%. A missing response counts as a miss. Check invented numbers and unlabelled causal claims separately; either fails its gate.

**Reviewer trial.** Eight practising life actuaries each review two different synthetic cases of matched difficulty, one using the plain Excel diff and the other using the tool. Each case contains seeded defects in both classes. Assign two reviewers to each sequence: A/tool then B/Excel, A/Excel then B/tool, B/tool then A/Excel, and B/Excel then A/tool. This gives each case and method equal representation in each position. Give reviewers the same task and underlying inputs for each case, withhold the answer key and give no feedback between runs.

Start timing when the reviewer opens the case materials and stop when they submit their first written list of issues for follow-up. For each reviewer calculate (Excel time − tool time) / Excel time. The median of the eight reductions must reach **35%**. Separately for each defect class, divide correctly identified seeded defects by opportunities presented under each method. The tool must match or exceed the Excel detection rate in **both** classes. A scorer uses the frozen key on anonymized findings with method labels removed. The owner signs off the times, counts and outcome; an incomplete trial leaves the gate open.

### Regulatory evidence and verification still needed

- The two cited implementing regulations describe different sources of evidence. 2023/894 concerns supervisory submissions. 2023/895 concerns SFCR disclosure; its S.05.01 includes premiums written and claims incurred. A workbook’s actual contents determine which comparisons it supports. [Supervisory reporting ITS](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2023/894/oj/eng/pdf) · [Public disclosure ITS](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32023R0895)
- EIOPA lists 2.8.2 for annual 2024 through annual 2026 and 2.10.0 from Q1 2027. Its final report lists S.29.01–S.29.04 among proposed deletions. The product excludes them for every supported filing period, independently of their legal filing status. [EIOPA taxonomy catalogue](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/tools-and-data/supervisory-reporting-dpm-and-xbrl_en) · [EIOPA-BoS-26/081, p. 20](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/document/download/7f1af01a-eb4e-4856-a5c5-c43bad02b754_en)
- For SCR eligibility, Article 82(1) requires eligible Tier 1 of at least half of SCR, specifies a strict upper bound of 15% of SCR for eligible Tier 3 and an inclusive upper bound of 50% for eligible Tier 2 and 3 combined. Article 82(3) requires restricted Tier 1 items (those in Article 69(a)(iii), 69(a)(v) and 69(b), and Tier 1 items under the transitional arrangement in Article 308b(9) of the Directive) to be less than 20% of total Tier 1. The article does not itself state how amounts above that limit are treated. The story’s shorthand “cap” leaves different equality cases. Step 2 must verify the applicable text for the chosen periods; D6 concerns what can be inferred from reported amounts at their stated precision. [EIOPA Single Rulebook, Article 82](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/rulebook/solvency-ii-single-rulebook/article-2512_en)
- **Step 2 evidence still due:** verify every cited row, column, template variant, LoB 31 label, sign, gross/net basis and metadata location against the exact filing’s official annotated templates. Check that overlapping measures really cover the same business and that both public and supervisory variants expose the detail a rule needs. Record discrepancies and unavailable inputs before authorizing a mapping. The illustrative movements, case outcomes and trial targets are acceptance examples or targets, not observed results.

## Handoff to Step 2

The scope, financial evidence rules and ratio allocation are settled for mapping. Verify the official 2.8.2 artifacts, including the optional 2.8.2 hotfix, and the coverage of each input variant. Confirm the Article 82 text in force for the supported periods and how S.23.01 reports restricted Tier 1 amounts above the Article 82(3) limit. Before freezing the Step 4 benchmark, agree numerical materiality thresholds for the verified measures. The benchmark scores and human trial results remain to be measured.

**Outside v1.** EIOPA applies 2.8.2 up to Q4/annual 2026 and 2.10.0 from the Q1 2027 reference period, and the Solvency II Review applies from 30 January 2027. The YE2026 to YE2027 pair therefore crosses taxonomy and framework, and R-002 blocks it. Whether v1.1 supports that comparison is an open roadmap decision for the owner; it does not affect v1 acceptance.
